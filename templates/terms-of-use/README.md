# Terms of Use

**Category:** Website & Application Governance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/terms-of-use)

## Overview

A foundational website Terms of Use agreement that sets the rules for accessing and using a company's site, outlining user obligations, permitted and prohibited activities, intellectual-property protections, liability limits, and a binding arbitration framework.

## What Are Terms of Use?

This document establishes the legal relationship between a company and its users by defining acceptable use, account requirements, intellectual-property protections, disclaimers, limitations of liability, and dispute-resolution procedures. It explains what users can and cannot do on the site, outlines the company's rights to modify or discontinue features, and incorporates related policies such as Privacy Policy and Cookie Notice. The template addresses third-party services, user interactions, feedback licensing, accessibility commitments, and includes a detailed arbitration agreement with class-action and jury-trial waivers.

## When You Need This Template

Terms of Use are necessary whenever a company operates a website, web application, or online service that users can access. They are especially important if the site:

- Allows account creation or user interactions
- Displays content or integrates with third-party tools
- Collects personal data or uses cookies and tracking technologies
- Relies on third-party services such as analytics, payment processors, or embedded content

## Why This Template

This template provides a comprehensive, modern, and legally informed structure for governing website access and use. It includes clear definitions of user obligations, detailed restrictions on misuse, intellectual-property protections, disclaimers, liability limitations, and robust dispute-resolution mechanisms. The template reflects current online-service practices and integrates seamlessly with Privacy Policy and Cookie Notice documents.

## Key Provisions

- Account creation and security requirements
- Access license and use restrictions, preventing misuse, reverse engineering, or commercial exploitation
- Company rights to modify, suspend, or discontinue the site
- Intellectual-property ownership and protection
- Feedback licensing granting the company broad rights to use user suggestions
- Integration of the Privacy Policy and Cookie Notice
- Indemnification obligations, requiring users to cover certain claims
- Third-party service disclaimers and rules for user interactions
- Comprehensive disclaimers of warranties and limitations of liability
- Termination rights, including account suspension or deletion
- State-specific consumer-rights notices
- Accessibility commitments aligned with WCAG 2.1 AA
- Detailed arbitration agreement, including class-action waiver, jury-trial waiver, opt-out rights, and alternative arbitration frameworks
