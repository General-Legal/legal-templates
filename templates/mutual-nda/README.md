# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**Category:** Agreements
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/mutual-non-disclosure-agreement-nda)

## Overview

A Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is a contract between two parties that governs how each side may access, use, and protect the other's confidential information when exploring a potential business relationship.

## When You Need This Template

This template applies when companies exchange sensitive information while evaluating business opportunities. Specific scenarios include:

- Sharing technical or product information
- Discussing financials or business plans
- Exploring partnerships or strategic collaborations
- Providing access to customer or employee data
- Reviewing prototypes, algorithms, or proprietary materials

## Why This Template

Created by attorneys with experience advising companies that routinely handle sensitive technical, commercial, and strategic information. It provides broad definitions of Confidential Information, strict use-and-disclosure limits, and clear obligations around handling, safeguarding, and returning materials while maintaining readability and practicality.

## Key Provisions

- Broad definition of Confidential Information
- Limits on use and disclosure
- Access restrictions for Representatives and Affiliates
- Standard confidentiality exceptions
- Procedures for legally compelled disclosures
- Return or destruction of materials at termination
- No IP rights granted; no reverse engineering
- Feedback license to the disclosing party
- Term, survival, and remedies for breach
