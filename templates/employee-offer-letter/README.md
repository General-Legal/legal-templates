# Employee Offer Letter (California)

**Category:** Agreements
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/employee-offer-letter)

## Overview

An Employee Offer Letter is a letter agreement between a company and a prospective employee that sets out the terms of the offer -- role, reporting line, work location, base salary, benefits, and at-will status -- and conditions the offer on signing a Confidential Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement. This template is drafted for California-based, full-time salaried exempt employees and reflects California-specific wage, hour, and arbitration law.

## When You Need This Template

This template applies when a company hires a salaried exempt employee whose work location is in California. Specific scenarios include:

- Extending a written offer to a full-time exempt hire
- Documenting base salary, payroll frequency, and benefits eligibility
- Confirming at-will employment and the absence of any fixed term
- Onboarding an employee who will have access to company proprietary information
- Hiring someone coming from a competitor or a prior employer with confidentiality obligations
- Offering employment subject to a reference check and U.S. work authorization

## Why This Template

Created by attorneys with experience advising California employers on hiring and employment documentation. It states the at-will relationship unambiguously, uses a flexible (non-accrued) vacation structure appropriate for exempt employees so there is no accrued balance to pay out at termination, addresses the prior-employer confidentiality risks that arise on lateral hires, and includes a JAMS arbitration agreement drafted around California's limits, carving out PAGA, FEHA, and Labor Code claims that cannot lawfully be compelled to arbitration.

## Key Provisions

- Position, duties, reporting line, and work location, with company discretion to modify
- Annual base salary, payroll deductions, and payroll frequency
- Benefits eligibility subject to plan documents and the employee handbook
- Flexible vacation for exempt employees, with no accrual, no carryover, and no payout on termination
- At-will employment, modifiable only by a signed writing from an officer of the company
- Compliance with company policies and required execution of the Employee Confidential Information and Inventions Assignment Agreement (Exhibit A)
- Restrictions on using or bringing in a prior employer's confidential information, and representations about pre-existing agreements
- Standard business hours, exempt classification, and no entitlement to overtime pay
- Binding, confidential JAMS arbitration under the Federal Arbitration Act, with jury-trial waiver and class, collective, and representative action waivers
- Excluded Claims carve-out for California PAGA, FEHA, and Labor Code claims that cannot be compelled to arbitration
- Company payment of arbitration fees exceeding court filing costs, and preserved right to seek injunctive relief in court
- Entire agreement, no-reliance, severability, and electronic signature and delivery provisions
- Offer contingent on a satisfactory reference check and verification of U.S. work authorization
- Signature blocks with acceptance deadline and anticipated start date
