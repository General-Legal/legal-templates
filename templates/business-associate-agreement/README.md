# Business Associate Agreement (BAA)

**Category:** Healthcare & Compliance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/business-associate-agreement-baa)

## Overview

A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is a legally mandated contract under HIPAA between a covered entity (healthcare provider or health plan) and a business associate -- any organization that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected health information (PHI) on their behalf.

## When You Need a BAA

This agreement is required whenever your company functions as a business associate under HIPAA. Common situations include:

- Offering cloud hosting or SaaS platforms to healthcare clients
- Performing data analytics on patient information
- Managing billing or claims processing
- Developing health-tech applications
- Supplying IT support to healthcare organizations

Operating without proper BAA documentation can trigger substantial financial penalties and legal exposure.

## Why This Template

This BAA template was created by attorneys with expertise in advising healthcare technology startups. It addresses essential HIPAA requirements while remaining practical and accessible. The template provides a solid foundation, though legal review before execution is always recommended.

## Key Provisions

- Permitted uses and disclosures of PHI
- Safeguard obligations and security requirements
- Breach notification procedures and timelines
- Subcontractor compliance requirements
- Return or destruction of PHI upon termination
- Individual rights and access to PHI
