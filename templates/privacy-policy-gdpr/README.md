# Privacy Policy (GDPR Enhanced)

**Category:** Privacy & Compliance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/privacy-policy-gdpr-enhanced)

## Overview

A GDPR-enhanced Privacy Policy explains how a company collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information across multiple jurisdictions, including the United States, European Economic Area (EEA), and United Kingdom. The template incorporates U.S.-required disclosures alongside additional transparency, rights, and legal-basis requirements imposed by GDPR and UK GDPR.

## What It Covers

The policy describes categories of personal information collected -- such as contact, demographic, profile, transactional, device, and online activity data -- along with information sources and processing purposes. These purposes include service delivery, personalization, analytics, marketing, compliance, and security.

### Distinguishing Features

Unlike U.S.-only templates, this includes:

- A dedicated Notice to European Users
- GDPR-style disclosures regarding lawful bases for processing and cross-border transfer mechanisms
- Expanded rights for European individuals

## When You Need This Template

Companies need this template when they operate in the United States but collect or process personal information from European individuals. This applies when:

- Websites or apps are accessible to European visitors
- Products or services are offered to EEA or UK residents
- User behavior in Europe is tracked or analyzed
- Global marketing campaigns are conducted
- Service providers process data in or from Europe

## Why This Template

The template provides a structured, multi-jurisdictional framework that satisfies both U.S. state privacy laws and GDPR/UK GDPR requirements in a single document, reducing the need for separate regional policies.

## Key Provisions

- Comprehensive data-collection disclosures
- Multiple data sources documentation
- Detailed processing purposes explanations
- GDPR-specific disclosures (lawful bases, enhanced transparency, expanded user rights)
- Cross-border transfer mechanisms and safeguards
- U.S. state privacy rights notice
- Tracking-technology disclosures
- User-choice mechanisms
- Security practices and limitations
- Children's privacy disclosures
- International data-transfer guidance for European users
