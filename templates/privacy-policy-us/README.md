# Privacy Policy (U.S. Only)

**Category:** Privacy & Compliance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/privacy-policy-u-s-only)

## Overview

This template explains how a company collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information specifically under U.S. privacy laws. It addresses data categories such as contact, demographic, transactional, device, and online activity information, along with purposes for collection including service delivery, personalization, analytics, marketing, security, and compliance.

## When You Need This Template

This template is appropriate for companies that:

- Operate in the United States and collect personal information from users, customers, or visitors
- Do not target or serve individuals in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom in ways triggering GDPR obligations
- Have compliance obligations driven by U.S. federal and state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, and similar statutes)
- Do not require GDPR-specific disclosures

## Why This Template

The template provides a structured framework for explaining data-handling practices in a clear, compliant manner. It incorporates modern data-collection practices, including cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and AI-powered features, while giving users clear management options.

## Key Provisions

- Categories of personal information collected
- Sources of personal information
- Explanations of how personal information is used
- Disclosures about sharing with service providers, partners, advisors, and business counterparties
- Description of cookies, pixels, and tracking technologies
- User choices and rights under U.S. state privacy laws
- Security practices and limitations
- Children's privacy protections under U.S. law
- Notice of changes and contact instructions
