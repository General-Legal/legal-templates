# Data Processing Addendum (DPA) -- U.S. and European Personal Data

**Category:** Privacy & Compliance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/data-processing-addendum-dpa-u-s-and-european-personal-data)

## Overview

A Data Processing Addendum is a contract that attaches to a customer agreement and governs how a service provider processes personal data on its customer's behalf. This is the global version: it covers U.S. state privacy laws and the GDPR, UK GDPR, and Swiss FADP in a single document, with a European Annex that incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and Swiss variations for cross-border transfers. A U.S.-only version, without the European annexes and transfer mechanisms, is available at [templates/dpa-us](../dpa-us).

## When You Need This Template

This template applies when a provider processes personal data for business customers across U.S. and European jurisdictions. Specific scenarios include:

- Signing enterprise or SaaS customers who require a DPA before purchase
- Processing personal data of individuals in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland
- Transferring personal data out of Europe and needing Standard Contractual Clauses in place
- Serving customers subject to the CCPA or other U.S. state privacy laws
- Engaging subprocessors, including cloud infrastructure and AI vendors
- Responding to customer security reviews, audits, or vendor-diligence questionnaires

## Why This Template

Drafted by attorneys who negotiate data processing terms on both the provider and customer side, it consolidates U.S. and European requirements into one addendum rather than separate regional agreements. The transfer mechanisms are pre-populated by reference, so the SCCs, UK Addendum, and Swiss terms take effect automatically where they apply, and the commercial provisions -- audit scope, assistance fees, subprocessor objection process -- are drafted to be workable for a provider while remaining defensible to enterprise counsel.

## Key Provisions

- Definitions aligned to both GDPR and U.S. state privacy law terminology
- Duration and scope, with processing surviving termination of the underlying agreement
- Processing limited to the customer's documented instructions
- Technical and organizational security measures, personnel confidentiality, and incident notification
- Allocation of customer-side security responsibilities and security assessment acknowledgement
- Data subject request assistance and handling of requests received by the provider
- Customer responsibilities for notices, consents, legal basis, and Restricted Data limits
- Subprocessor authorization, notice, flow-down obligations, and objection rights
- Audit rights, including acceptance of SOC 2, ISO, or NIST reports in lieu of an on-site audit
- Return, deletion, or anonymization of personal data after cessation
- AI and automated processing restrictions, including no model training on personal data
- Annex 1 processing details, populating the SCC appendices
- Annex 2 European Annex covering DPIAs, prior consultation, and EU, UK, and Swiss restricted transfers
- Annex 3 State Privacy Laws Annex with service provider and contractor certifications
- Annex 4 security measures and Annex 5 subprocessor list
