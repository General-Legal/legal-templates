# One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

**Category:** Agreements
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/one-way-non-disclosure-agreement-nda)

## Overview

A one-way NDA is a contract where one party (the Company) discloses confidential information while the other party (the Recipient) agrees to maintain confidentiality and limit use to defined business purposes. This template protects sensitive technical, commercial, financial, or product information.

## When You Need This Template

You need a one-way NDA when your company is the only party disclosing confidential information while another party evaluates a potential business relationship. Common scenarios include:

- Sharing product roadmaps or prototypes
- Providing access to internal data or technology
- Discussing financials or business plans
- Allowing prospective partners, vendors, or investors to review sensitive materials

## Why This Template

Drafted by attorneys experienced in advising companies that routinely share sensitive information with prospective partners, vendors, or investors. It provides practical protections while remaining readable and includes modern safeguards against reverse engineering and AI/machine-learning misuse.

## Key Provisions

- Broad definition of Company Confidential Information
- Use and disclosure limits (Permitted Use only)
- Access restrictions for Representatives and Affiliates
- Standard confidentiality exceptions
- Legally compelled disclosure procedures
- Material return or destruction at termination
- No IP rights granted; reverse engineering and AI-model use prohibited
- Term, survival, and breach remedies
