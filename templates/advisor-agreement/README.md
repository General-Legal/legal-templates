# Advisor Agreement

**Category:** Agreements
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/advisor-agreement)

## Overview

An Advisor Agreement is a contract between a company and an individual advisor that sets the terms on which the advisor provides consulting and advisory services, typically in exchange for a stock option grant rather than cash compensation, while assigning ownership of any resulting work product to the company.

## When You Need This Template

This template applies when a company engages an individual to provide ongoing strategic, technical, or scientific guidance outside an employment relationship. Specific scenarios include:

- Appointing a formal advisor or advisory board member
- Compensating an advisor with an equity grant instead of cash
- Engaging an academic or clinical expert affiliated with a university or research institute
- Granting an advisor access to confidential product, technical, or financial information
- Naming an advisor publicly on a website, pitch deck, or promotional materials

## Why This Template

Created by attorneys with experience structuring advisory relationships for early-stage and growth companies. It pairs an option-based compensation structure with strong IP assignment and confidentiality terms, addresses the third-party and institutional conflicts that commonly arise with expert advisors, and keeps the advisor's independent-contractor status unambiguous while remaining short enough to sign quickly.

## Key Provisions

- Description of the Services, with a field-specific non-compete during the engagement
- Stock option grant as the sole consideration, subject to Board approval and a vesting schedule
- Reimbursement of pre-approved, documented out-of-pocket expenses
- Assignment of Inventions to the company, with a carve-out and license for the advisor's Preexisting IP
- Waiver of moral rights and a power of attorney to perfect the company's IP rights
- Broad Proprietary Information confidentiality obligations, with standard exceptions
- Privacy and data protection commitments, including CCPA/CPRA compliance
- Procedures for legally compelled disclosures and DTSA whistleblower immunity notice
- Return or destruction of confidential materials on termination, with written certification
- At-will termination by either party, plus termination after 12 months of inactivity, and survival of Sections 2 through 9
- Independent contractor status, with the advisor responsible for taxes and ineligible for benefits
- Company rights to use the advisor's name, likeness, and biography, subject to endorsement limits
- No-conflicts representations and compliance with Institution Policies, including advance notice of required disclosures
