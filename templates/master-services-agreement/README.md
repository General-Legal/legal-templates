# Master Services Agreement (MSA)

**Category:** Agreements
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/master-services-agreement-msa)

## Overview

A Master Services Agreement is the umbrella contract between a software company and its customers, setting the standing legal terms — license scope, data rights, fees, warranties, liability, confidentiality, and termination — under which each individual Order Form is signed. It is drafted for a company that deploys a software platform into a customer environment and connects it to the customer's third-party accounts and integrations.

## When You Need This Template

This template applies whenever a company sells software or services to business customers on recurring, order-by-order terms. Specific scenarios include:

- Licensing a software platform to enterprise or business customers
- Deploying a platform inside a customer's own environment or infrastructure
- Connecting a product to a customer's third-party accounts, APIs, or integrations
- Selling on a subscription basis with renewal terms and tiered user pricing
- Layering professional services, implementation, or onboarding onto a software subscription
- Standing up repeat business with a single master contract and successive Order Forms

## Why This Template

Created by attorneys with experience advising software companies that deploy their products into customer environments and depend on third-party integrations. It separates the durable legal framework from the commercial terms that change deal to deal, so the master agreement is signed once and each new engagement is papered with an Order Form. The document allocates ownership and data rights carefully — company IP, Integration Tools, Aggregated Data, and Customer Data and Outputs each addressed on their own terms — while keeping restrictions, disclaimers, and remedies workable for a sales process.

## Key Provisions

- Definitions covering the Platform, Services, Documentation, Authorized Users, Connected Accounts, Integration Tools, Customer Data, and Aggregated Data
- Order Form structure, with precedence rules between the Agreement and each Order
- Platform license grant, conditioned on payment of Fees and compliance with the Agreement
- Connected Accounts terms, including customer representations and a disclaimer of third-party availability
- Maintenance and support obligations, with the customer responsible for the Customer Environment
- Personal Data handling through a required Data Processing Addendum, plus Third-Party Services terms
- Customer restrictions, setup responsibilities, and a right to audit the Customer Environment on notice
- Company Intellectual Property, Generic Tools, and Feedback licensing
- Aggregated Data rights and Customer Data and Outputs responsibilities
- Fees and expenses, taxes, ACH payment terms, late interest, and suspension for overdue accounts
- Mutual warranties plus disclaimers covering the Platform, Outputs, and Connected Accounts
- Limitation of liability, with excluded liability carve-outs and a fees-based damages cap
- Mutual confidentiality obligations, standard exceptions, and protection requirements
- Indemnification by both parties, with a defined claim procedure
- Term, renewal, termination for breach or with no outstanding Orders, and post-termination obligations
- Governing law, informal dispute resolution, and binding AAA arbitration
- Miscellaneous terms including publicity, assignment, force majeure, notices, and entire agreement
- Exhibit A Order Form and Exhibit B Customer Supplemental Terms

## Before You Use This Template

This document is a starting point, not a finished contract. Review the following before sending it to a customer:

- Complete every highlighted field: the Effective Date and party names in the preamble, the governing-law state and arbitration venue in *Governing Law* and *Dispute Resolution*, the Company email address used in *Dispute Resolution* and *Notices*, and the signature blocks
- Fill in Exhibit A (Order Form #1) — anticipated start date, billing information, payment and billing method, Initial and Renewal Term lengths, the Services and Restrictions rows, and the Fees amount
- Decide whether to keep the highlighted additional user tier language in Exhibit A, and set the block size and per-block annual pricing if you do
- Confirm the *Term and Termination* defaults work for your business: the one-year Initial Order Term, automatic renewal, thirty days' notice to terminate with no outstanding Orders, and the thirty-day cure period for breach
- Check the liability cap in *Amount of Damages* — twelve months of Fees paid or payable — and the *Exclusion of Damages* carve-outs for confidentiality breaches, IP misappropriation, gross negligence or willful misconduct, and indemnification obligations; these carve-outs are uncapped and are the terms customers most often negotiate
- Review the payment defaults in *Fees and Expenses; Payments*: ACH-only payment, the thirty-day past-due threshold for interest and suspension, and the Net 60 payment term shown on the Order Form
- Adapt the *Definitions* section to your actual product. The definition of **"Platform"** is left as a highlighted field and must be filled in with a description of your own product. **"Connected Accounts"**, **"Connected Account Data"**, and **"Integration Tools"** assume an integration-heavy product that links to customer third-party accounts — if that does not describe your offering, delete those definitions and the provisions that depend on them (*Connected Accounts* under *Provision of Services*, the *Connected Accounts* disclaimer under *Warranties and Disclaimers*, and the Integration Tools references in *Company Intellectual Property* and *Remedies*)
- Confirm the source-code delivery language in *Company Intellectual Property* and the customer-deployment model in *Setup Responsibilities* match how you actually ship — both assume the customer installs and operates the Platform in its own environment
- Put the Data Processing Addendum referenced in *Personal Data* in place, and either populate Exhibit B with the customer rider or delete it
