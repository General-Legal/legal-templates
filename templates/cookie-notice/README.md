# Cookie Notice

**Category:** Privacy & Compliance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/cookie-notice)

## Overview

A Cookie Notice explains how a company uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on its websites and mobile apps. It describes various cookie types -- essential, analytics, functionality, advertising, and social -- and identifies whether they are first-party or third-party. The notice also covers related technologies such as web beacons, Flash LSOs, browser storage, SDKs, and session-replay tools.

## When You Need This Template

Organizations require a Cookie Notice whenever their digital properties use cookies or similar tracking technologies that collect information from users' devices, whether for:

- Essential site functionality
- Analytics
- Advertising
- Social media integrations
- Email-tracking pixels
- Mobile SDKs
- Session-replay tools

Many privacy regulations mandate clear disclosure and user consent for non-essential cookies.

## Why This Template

This template offers a comprehensive, legally informed framework for explaining your site's cookie and tracking practices in a clear, user-friendly way. It provides straightforward definitions, categorized cookie tables, and details on opt-out mechanisms through cookie banners, dashboards, browser settings, and advertising opt-outs, serving as a practical compliance starting point.

## Key Provisions

- Definition of cookies and operational mechanics
- First-party versus third-party cookie distinctions
- Cookie categorization (advertising, analytics, essential, functionality, social)
- Provider identification table with user control options
- Coverage of related tracking technologies
- User preference management mechanisms
- Notice regarding functionality impact of cookie disabling
- Reference to the organization's Privacy Policy
