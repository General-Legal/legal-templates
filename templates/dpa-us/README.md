# Data Processing Addendum (DPA) -- U.S. Personal Data

**Category:** Privacy & Compliance
**Format:** Microsoft Word (.DOCX)
**Source:** [General Legal](https://general.legal/library/data-processing-addendum-dpa)

## Overview

This template is a Data Processing Addendum for U.S. personal data that attaches to and forms part of an existing customer agreement, setting out how a service provider may process personal data on a customer's behalf. It defines the parties' respective roles, limits processing to the customer's documented instructions, and addresses security measures, subprocessors, audits, data subject requests, and the return or deletion of data. A separate global version covering both U.S. and European personal data is available at `templates/dpa-global/`.

## When You Need This Template

This template is appropriate for companies that:

- Provide a service that involves processing personal data on behalf of business customers
- Are asked by a customer to sign a DPA alongside a services agreement, order form, or MSA
- Are subject to U.S. state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, Virginia CDPA, Colorado CPA, and similar statutes) as a service provider, contractor, or processor
- Do not process personal data subject to the GDPR or UK GDPR, and therefore do not need standard contractual clauses or international transfer terms
- Use AI or machine learning features and need clear commitments about whether customer data may be used for model training

## Why This Template

Drafted by attorneys for U.S.-only data processing relationships, the addendum keeps the obligations proportionate to that scope rather than importing European transfer mechanisms that do not apply. It pairs standard processor commitments with practical operational limits — a defined audit process that accepts SOC 2 or ISO reports in lieu of on-site audits, cost allocation for assistance beyond the services, and a restriction on submitting sensitive data to the services — and includes modern terms addressing AI training and automated decision-making.

## Key Provisions

- Definitions aligned to U.S. state privacy law terminology
- Duration and scope, surviving expiration of the underlying agreement
- Processing limited to the customer's documented instructions
- Technical and organizational security measures, personnel confidentiality, and information security incident notification
- Allocation of the customer's own security responsibilities and security assessment
- Assistance with data subject requests and handling of requests received directly
- Customer representations regarding notices, consents, and Restricted Data
- Subprocessor authorization, flow-down obligations, and an opportunity to object to new subprocessors
- Annual audit rights with an agreed audit plan and third-party report alternative
- Return, deletion, or anonymization of personal data after cessation of services
- Restrictions on using personal data to train or improve AI models, and automated decision-making disclosures
- Liability cap tied to the underlying agreement, and conflict and amendment terms
- Annex 1 (Data Processing Details), Annex 2 (State Privacy Laws Annex), Annex 3 (Security Measures), and Annex 4 (List of Subprocessors)
